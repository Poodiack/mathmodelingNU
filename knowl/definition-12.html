Definition 8.3.1.

A discrete logistic equation (also called a logistic map or a constrained growth model) is an equation of the form

\begin{equation*} a_{n+1} = a_n + b(c-a_n)a_n \end{equation*}

where \(b\) and \(c\) are constants. This type of equation is often used to model population growth where \(a_n\) is the population at time \(n\text{.}\) The constant \(b\) is called the intrinsic growth rate and \(c\) is called the carrying capacity.

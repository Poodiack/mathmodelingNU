Row-reduce the augmented matrix to its RREF to solve the system:

\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} 2x + y + 3z & = 10 \\ x + y + z & = 6 \\ x + 3y + 2z & = 13 \end{aligned} \end{equation*}

The augmented matrix is

\begin{equation*} \left[\begin{array}{rrr|r} 2 & 1 & 3 & 10 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & 6 \\ 1 & 3 & 2 & 13 \end{array}\right] \end{equation*}

First we perform the row operations \(R_1-2R_2\to R_2\) and \(R_1-2R_3\to R_3\text{:}\)

\begin{equation*} \left[\begin{array}{rrr|r} 2 & 1 & 3 & 10 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 & -2 \\ 0 & -5 & -1 & -16 \end{array}\right] \end{equation*}

Next we perform \(-5R_2+R_3 \to R_3\text{:}\)

\begin{equation*} \left[\begin{array}{rrr|r} 2 & 1 & 3 & 10 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & -6 & -6 \end{array}\right] \end{equation*}

Then we perform \(R_1+R_2\to R_1\text{,}\) \(-R_2\to R_2\text{,}\) and \(-(1/6)R_3\to R_3\text{:}\)

\begin{equation*} \left[\begin{array}{rrr|r} 2 & 0 & 4 & 8 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right] \end{equation*}

Lastly, we perform \((1/2)R_1 - 2R_3\to R_1\) and \(R_2+R_3\to R_2\text{:}\)

\begin{equation*} \left[\begin{array}{rrr|r} 1 & 0 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right] \end{equation*}

This final matrix is in RREF and we see the solution is \(x = 2\text{,}\) \(y = 3\text{,}\) and \(z = 1\text{.}\)

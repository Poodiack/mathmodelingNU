Item 2.

It may be difficult, costly, or dangerous to collect data for creating an empirical model. Consider a hospital emergency room. If we were interested in modeling the waiting time of patients in terms of the number of doctors on staff, we could vary the number of doctors from week-to-week, collect data on waiting time, and construct an empirical model. This approach would certainly take much time and could result in dead patients. A much more practical approach is to simulate the behavior of the emergency room on a computer and vary the number of doctors in the simulation.

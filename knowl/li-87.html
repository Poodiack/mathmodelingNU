Item 4.

System may contain random events that we do not want to over-simplify. Consider a check-out line at a supermarket where customers arrive at an average of 2 per minute and the cashier can service an average of 3 customers per minute. If we wanted to model the waiting time of customers, we might be tempted to say that they won’t have to wait at all because the service rate is greater than the arrival rate. This is a vast oversimplification. Instead, we will simulate the arrival and service of customers and analyze waiting time.

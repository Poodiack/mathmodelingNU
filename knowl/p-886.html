Consider a cup of coffee that is initially \(100^\circ\)F, cools to \(90^\circ\)F in 10 minutes, and sits in a room whose temperature is a constant \(60^\circ\)F. A simple assumption for how the coffee cools is that the amount the temperature changes from one minute to the next is proportional to the difference between the coffee temperature and the room temperature.
